YelloTrace

AN INDEPENDENT PROJECT BY CNRCO

YelloTrace is an independent cybersecurity project focused on digital surface security analysis. We examine the public facing parts of websites and online infrastructure to identify potential exposures, visible misconfigurations, and security signals that may deserve closer attention.

The goal is to make external security easier to understand. Through careful observation and evidence led analysis, YelloTrace aims to help website owners recognise what is visible from the outside and understand where further verification or improvement may be useful.

Our interest lies in web security, digital forensics, and the investigation of overlooked details. Each review should have a clearly defined scope and appropriate authorisation, with findings described carefully and without overstating what the evidence shows.
