YelloTrace

AN INDEPENDENT PROJECT BY CNRCO

YelloTrace is an independent cybersecurity project focused on digital surface security analysis. We examine the public-facing parts of websites and online infrastructure to identify potential exposures, visible misconfigurations, and security signals that may deserve closer attention.

The goal is to make external security easier to understand. Through careful observation and evidence-led analysis, YelloTrace aims to help website owners recognise what is visible from the outside and where further verification or improvement may be useful.

Any review should have a clearly defined scope and appropriate authorisation. Findings should be described carefully, supported by evidence, and should not overstate what an external observation can establish.
